Citation Readiness Audit

Category: Content and GEO. Framework: Proof of Importance.

Audits whether content contains extractable atomic claims that an LLM would cite. It also checks whether those claims are unique to the source. It then scores whether structural coherence supports clean cluster-level extraction.

The audit runs eight steps. It produces a weighted citation readiness score out of 100.

The six scored components are:

  1. Atomic Claim Density (25 percent): Count of self-contained, declarative, sub-20-word factual statements
  2. Claim Uniqueness (20 percent): Whether each claim is unique, better-stated, or commodity
  3. Structural Accessibility (20 percent): Ten-point checklist covering headings, definition blocks, tables, FAQ, and schema
  4. Structural Coherence (15 percent): Bias and modularity scoring through KeywordGraph
  5. Evidence Density (10 percent): Evidence elements divided by total paragraphs
  6. Query-Response Alignment (10 percent): Whether target queries get direct, front-loaded, atomic answers

Tools required: KeywordGraph and Firecrawl.

Triggers: check citation readiness, audit LLM citability, assess AI extractability, PoI audit, extractability check.

Hosted at: https://seo-skills.md/citation-readiness-audit

Competitive Gap Mapper

Category: Research and GEO.

Maps relation-level positioning gaps between a brand and three to five competitors. It builds a merged competitor corpus graph, then runs bidirectional difference analysis against the brand graph.

The output separates four territory types:

  1. Shared territory: Commodity claims every competitor makes. Table stakes, not differentiation.
  2. Competitor-owned territory: Positioning competitors hold that the brand does not
  3. Brand-owned territory: Unique positioning the brand should defend
  4. Unclaimed white space: Entity space no competitor occupies

Tools required: KeywordGraph and Firecrawl.

Triggers: map competitive gaps, positioning audit, entity-level analysis, positioning white space.

Hosted at: https://seo-skills.md/competitive-gap-mapper

Entity Map Builder

Category: Technical and GEO.

Generates a conforming entitymap.json file and its entitymap.html companion. The files map the entities, relationships, and topical coverage that define a brand's knowledge graph footprint.

The skill implements the EntityMap v1.0 open standard. That standard was created by Fred Laurent and Dixon Jones at entitymap.org. Jack Boutchard authored the Claude Skill that builds files conforming to it.

Triggers: entity mapping, knowledge graph, entity-first SEO, topical authority.

Hosted at: https://entitymap.md/

Frameworks Encoded in the Skills

The skills on seo-skills.md are not generic prompts. Each one operationalizes a named framework authored by Jack Boutchard.

Proof of Importance (PoI)

The Proof of Importance framework identifies seven signals that determine whether an LLM cites content. The name borrows from the NEM blockchain's consensus mechanism.

The signals operate in two tiers. Retrieval gates decide whether content is surfaced at all. Citation scoring decides whether surfaced content gets cited.

Tier one, retrieval gates:

  1. Semantic Relevance: How closely the content's meaning aligns with the query
  2. Structural Accessibility: How easily the content can be parsed, chunked, and extracted

Tier two, citation scoring:

  1. Source Authority: The perceived credibility and expertise of the publishing domain
  2. Entity Relationships: How well the content connects to established knowledge graph entities
  3. Evidence Density: The concentration of citations, data points, and verifiable claims
  4. Corroboration: Whether the same facts appear across multiple independent sources
  5. Recency: How recently the content was published or updated

The Citation Readiness Audit skill scores content against these signals directly.

EGOS (Exalt Growth Operating System)

EGOS is a four-system, twelve-module operating system for building AI visibility. Every module connects to revenue outcomes rather than vanity metrics.

The four systems are:

  1. Intelligence: Research, entity mapping, and competitive analysis
  2. Content: Production, structure, and citation readiness
  3. Amplification: Third-party corroboration and off-site signals
  4. Optimization: Measurement, iteration, and technical refinement

Entity-First Framework

The Entity-First framework treats brand visibility as an entity graph problem rather than a keyword problem. It builds visibility through entity density and knowledge graph connections.

The four pillars are:

  1. Foundation: Canonical entity definitions and disambiguation
  2. Structure: Site architecture that mirrors entity relationships
  3. Signals: Corroboration and authority across third-party sources
  4. Measurement: Citation tracking across AI platforms

The Entity Map Builder skill implements this framework at the file level.
